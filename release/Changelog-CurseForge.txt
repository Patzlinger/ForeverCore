## 0.44b update – October 20, 2026

Forever Core is growing from a set of single addons into one interface: one settings window, one minimap button, one look, and Questie, Details!, Masque and TomTom built right in.

### Three new addons (beta)
- **ForeverPlates – nameplates that tell you what matters.** Replaces the enemy nameplates.
  - Health in percent and total, an execute color you choose yourself
  - Threat as a word and in percent ("Aggro 87 %")
  - A cast bar as wide as the health bar, with the cast's target written small below it ("Target: Name"). Interrupted casts briefly show "Interrupted by Name", whether it was a kick or crowd control.
  - Your own DoTs above the bar, crowd control at the side, combo points on top of the bar, raid markers and a quest marker for quest mobs (with Questie)
  - Plates stack neatly and stay sharp at any distance. Can follow the ForeverFrames look.
  - Simple friendly nameplates, optional
  - Settings: /fplates
- **ForeverAlts – all your characters at a glance.**
  - Level and XP, rested XP (estimated while logged out), gold with a total, location, professions, played time, mail (with expiry), honor and last login – turn every column on or off
  - The recipes of all your characters with a search, and "Can be made by" in item tooltips
  - Show the bags and bank of any character (with ForeverBags). Click your gold in ForeverBags or use its own key binding.
  - With ForeverQuests: quest tooltips and the "Entering dungeon" hint show which of your alts have the quest too
  - Settings: /falts
- **ForeverTooltip – Blizzard's tooltip in the Forever look.**
  - Dark and flat with a fine gold border, the border in item quality or class color if you like
  - Item level, item and spell IDs, the unit's target, player names in class colors, a flat health bar
  - At the mouse or at an anchor you can move
  - Settings: /ftip

### One interface instead of many addons
- **One settings window for everything:** a list on the left, grouped by area (Frames, Bars, Navigation, Comfort, Helpers) and showing only what you have installed, and one addon at a time on the right. The first page, **"Forever Core » Overview"**, shows all addons as tiles: click a tile to turn an addon on or off.
- **Search all settings:** the "Search settings …" field at the top right finds any setting of any Forever addon, opens the right page and highlights it.
- **One minimap button:** it opens the settings window (Shift-click: the overview, right-click: move all frames). The small buttons of the single addons are gone.
- **ForeverHelpers – three helpers in one addon:** ForeverLoot (loot rolls), ForeverBuffs (buff reminder) and ForeverVendor (merchant helper) are now one addon. Same settings, same commands (/floot, /fbuffs, /fvendor). If you still have the old folders, they are turned off automatically – please delete them.
- **Works with the addons you already use:** Forever Core recognizes other popular addons for the same job (e.g. Bartender4, Dominos, Bagnon, Baganator, AdiBags, Cell, Grid2, VuhDo, Shadowed Unit Frames, Prat, SexyMap, Quartz, Kaliel's Tracker, TipTac, Plater, BetterBlizzPlates). For each pair you choose which one runs; you can switch back any time ("Other addons …" in the overview). **Leatrix Plus** keeps running – only the features a Forever addon takes over are switched off.
- **Details! in the Forever look by default** (once per character, as a copy "Forever Core" of your profile – your own profile stays untouched; switch back in the overview).
- **Masque support** for the buttons of ForeverBars and the slots of ForeverBags.
- **Shorter player names:** Forever players have a first and a last name. Frames, nameplates, loot rolls and whisper windows now show "Hexeron S." – or choose "first name only" or "full name" in the overview (General » Player names). Tooltip, chat and the character overview keep the full name.
- **Lighter in the background:** all addons share one timer, and every task only runs when it is needed.
- A "Forever Hub" button in the game menu (Escape), right below "Macros".

### ForeverQuests with Questie
- Questie's own tracker is switched off automatically; its map icons and tooltips keep working and follow the quests you track. Blizzard's own quest markers no longer show up next to Questie's.
- Right-click a quest for Questie actions: highlight it on the map, show the turn-in, set a TomTom waypoint (with TomTom), copy the Wowhead link.
- **Quest log in the Forever look:** the world map gets the flat Forever frame, and the quest list next to it uses the ForeverQuests lines, filters, pins and "Together". Click a quest for a details page with objectives, rewards and buttons to share, pin, abandon or suggest it to your group. With Questie it also shows the whole quest chain and only the markers of that quest on the map. The map can be moved and resized freely.
- **Dungeon window:** with Questie, all quests of every dungeon come from Questie's database – quest giver, location, level, prerequisites (marked "do this first"), rewards and chains. "Group" shows who in your group has which quest, has finished it or is missing it.
- "Entering dungeon" hint with your group's progress on the dungeon quests.
- The filter menu works the other way round now: everything is shown, untick what you want to hide.
- A slim line at the bottom of the quest log shows how many quests are ready to turn in and how much XP they give.
- Quest IDs are hidden by default ("Show quest IDs" in /fq).

### Improved
- ForeverUnits: the "Forever" skin got a full makeover – fine edges with a gold line, muted bars with a soft shine, gold corner brackets for elites and bosses (silver for rares). Elite, rare and boss enemies also get Blizzard's dragon or star icon at the target and focus frame.
- ForeverUnits: DoT bars only show on targets you can attack.
- ForeverBags: the bank opens in the middle of the screen with your bags docked right next to it; move it once and it remembers the spot. New option "Show bags separately". New items shimmer gold instead of blue, quest starters show the yellow exclamation mark, and the bags open with their corner at your mouse.
- ForeverVendor: selling junk and repairing work on their own – whenever they are on, they run at every merchant. The "Automatic" switch now only decides whether restocking happens by itself or via the "Buy" button.
- ForeverBars: new look for the experience and reputation bars, and the bars line up exactly with action bar 1. The marker bar shows "Marks / Pings" in its title (click to switch between target and world markers).
- ForeverMinimap: the mail tile uses Blizzard's clean envelope, greyed out without mail and in color with a gold edge when mail arrives. The button strip is always visible by default.
- ForeverChat: whispers also show in the normal chat by default, and the whisper window shows who you are writing to.
- ForeverFrames: Blizzard's raid manager is hidden by default.
- ForeverQuests, "Together": shared quests look like the rest of the tracker and sit in one "Together" box.
- Settings pages: even spacing and tidy boxes on every page, no more overlapping labels.

### Fixed
- ForeverBags: bag and bank slots no longer end up behind their window (dark and not clickable), and the bank shows exactly the bank slots you really have.
- ForeverQuests: the scroll bar in the quest log works again.
- ForeverVendor: grey items are sold on the first visit to a merchant, even before the game has loaded their prices.
- The "Forever Hub" button in the game menu no longer causes "blocked action" errors.
- ForeverMinimap: map icons of other addons (e.g. Questie) no longer end up in the button strip.
- ForeverQuests, "Together": you no longer show up twice.
- ForeverQuests: quests you haven't picked up yet show their rewards; the dungeon window no longer breaks when Questie or AtlasLoot is missing or outdated.

### Good to know
- The release zip now contains only the addon folders – copy them into `Interface\AddOns`.
- **ForeverLoot, ForeverBuffs and ForeverVendor** are now part of ForeverHelpers. You can delete their old folders.

## 0.44b – October 18, 2026

### Four new addons
- **ForeverBars – action bars in the Forever look.** Replaces Blizzard's action bars with clean, flat bars.
  - Action bars, pet bar, stance bar and a totem bar for Shamans, each with its own size, layout, visibility, fading and key bindings (/fb)
  - Copy the settings of one action bar to another with one click (its position stays)
  - **Experience and reputation bars** (formerly ForeverXP): slim bars with a fine gold border, as wide as action bar 1 and right below it. XP per hour, time to level-up, rested XP and the XP your completed quests will give you. Settings: /fb (or /fxp)
  - **Marker bar:** target and world markers in a 3×3 grid, plus ready check, role poll and countdown, all with their own key bindings shown on the buttons
  - Profession bar, a bag bar and a micro menu that docks right below the minimap
  - The profession and marker bars fold into a small arrow button when you need the space, and fold out again even in combat
  - Leaves your bars alone if you use Bartender4

## 0.1b – October 14, 2026

Welcome to the first public beta of **Forever Core** for World of Warcraft: Forever!

*Less clutter. More control. Just the essentials.*

Forever Core is a small set of lightweight addons that cover the basics, look clean, and stay out of your way. Use one, a few, or all of them – each works on its own.

### What's included

**ForeverFrames – party and raid frames**
- Clean, compact frames with click casting
- Incoming heals and shields at a glance
- Debuffs with dispel type, so you know what you can remove
- Your HoTs and those of other players in the group
- Pet frames with an aggro border
- Icons for incoming resurrections and summons
- Optional: sort your group by role

**ForeverUnits – player, target, target of target, focus and pet**
- Class icon or portrait, level and elite/rare marker
- PvP and resting icons
- Optional DoT bars for your target and focus – great for Warlocks and Shadow Priests
- Can match the look of ForeverFrames with a single click

**ForeverCast – cast bar**
- A clean cast bar with your latency and spell queue window
- Shows the global cooldown

**ForeverLoot – loot rolls**
- See who picked Need, Greed or Pass, what everyone rolled, and who won
- Disappears on its own a few seconds after the result

**ForeverXP – experience tracking**
- XP per hour and estimated time to your next level
- See how much XP your finished quests are worth before you turn them in

**ForeverBuffs – buffs and consumables**
- Shows a small icon only when something is missing – never in your way (can be hidden in combat)
- Your own buffs, and whether the highest rank is active – click an icon to cast it
- Group buffs: who is missing your Fortitude, Intellect or Mark of the Wild, and which buffs your group could give you
- Food and drink in your bags – and whether there is something better for your level
- Add your own list, e.g. "Healing Potion:3", plus ammo, soul shards, weapon poisons/oils and shaman weapon imbues
With two or more Forever Core addons installed, their minimap buttons are bundled into a single Forever Core button.

Available in English and German.

### Good to know
- This is a beta – thanks for helping us test it!
- WoW: Forever is a beta too. Sometimes it forgets your settings after logging in. This is a game issue, and your settings come back once it is fixed.
- The DoT bars are still experimental and may look slightly different depending on your game version.
- HoTs from other players are recognized for the most common spells. If one is missing, let us know which one.

### Found a bug or have an idea?
We'd love to hear from you! The addon **BugSack** shows the exact error, so please include it in your report.
