# Forever Core

*Less clutter. More control. Just the essentials.*

Forever Core is a small set of lightweight addons for **World of Warcraft: Forever**. They cover the basics, look clean and stay out of your way. Use one, a few or all of them, because each one works on its own.

All addons share one flat, dark look with gold accents. They're available in English and German.

---

## Pick what you need
Install the package and log in. The **Forever Core Hub** opens once and lets you tick the addons you want, then click "Reload". You can change your choice at any time with **/forever**.

Every addon needs the small base addon **ForeverCore**. It's included in the package and in every single-addon download.

---

## The addons

**ForeverFrames – party and raid frames**
- Clean, compact frames with click casting
- Incoming heals and shields at a glance
- Debuffs with their dispel type, so you know what you can remove
- Your HoTs and those of other players
- Pet frames with an aggro border
- Icons for incoming resurrections and summons
- Optional: sort your group by role
- Settings: /ff

**ForeverUnits – player, target, target of target, focus and pet**
- Class icon or portrait, level and elite/rare marker
- PvP and resting icons
- Optional DoT bars for your target and focus, great for Warlocks and Shadow Priests. You can detach them and place them anywhere.
- Takes over the ForeverFrames look with a single click
- Settings: /fu

**ForeverCast – cast bar**
- A clean cast bar with your latency, lag zone and spell queue window
- Shows the global cooldown
- Settings: /fcast

**ForeverLoot – loot rolls**
- See who picked Need, Greed or Pass, what everyone rolled and who won
- Disappears on its own a few seconds after the result
- Settings: /floot

**ForeverXP – experience bar**
- A slim bar in Blizzard style that takes the place of the default XP bar
- Your level, XP per hour, time to level-up, and the XP your completed quests will give you
- Hover over it to see mobs and quests left until level-up, plus rested XP
- A slimmer **reputation bar** in the same style sits next to it and shows your watched faction: standing, progress, reputation per hour and the time to the next rank
- Prefer a box? The classic style is still there
- Settings: /fxp

**ForeverBuffs – buffs and consumables**
- Shows a small icon only when something is missing
- Your own buffs, and whether the highest rank is active. Click an icon to cast it.
- Group buffs: who is missing your buffs, and which buffs your group could give you
- Food and drink in your bags, and whether there is something better for your level
- Your own list, e.g. "Healing Potion:3", plus ammo, soul shards, poisons/oils and weapon imbues
- Settings: /fbuffs

**ForeverVendor – merchant helper**
- Sells your grey items and repairs if you want (guild bank first, if you like)
- Restocks class reagents, the best food and drink for your level, fitting ammo and your own list
- A small window at the merchant shows your shopping list with prices. One click on "Buy" and you're done.
- A spending limit per visit and a gold reserve keep your wallet safe
- Settings: /fvendor

**ForeverChat – your chat, just cleaner** *(new)*
- Keeps Blizzard's chat (whispers, channels, links and communities work as usual), with a transparent window and flat tabs
- A small button bar right in the tab row
- Player names in class colors with their level
- Short channel names, clickable web links and highlighted mentions of your name
- Instant messaging: an "IM" tab with one sub-tab per person, which blinks gold when you have unread messages
- The input line only shows up while you're typing
- Copy the whole chat with one click
- Settings: /fchat

**ForeverSpellbook – compact spellbook** *(new)*
- All your spells in tabs, with a search
- Tells you when your bars still have a lower rank of a spell, and fixes it with one click
- Shows what your trainer has next and what it costs
- Can replace the spellbook key (P)
- Settings: /fsb

## Made to work together
- **One minimap button:** with two or more Forever Core addons, their buttons are bundled into a single Forever Core button.
- **Forever Core Hub:** type **/forever** for a list of all your Forever Core addons, with their commands and a button that takes you straight to their settings. You'll also find Forever Core in Blizzard's options (Esc » Options » AddOns) and in the addon menu at the minimap.
- **Move everything at once:** type **/fmove** to unlock all frames together, then place them with a grid and snapping (hold Shift to move freely). Type /fmove again to lock them.
- **Exact positions:** X/Y fields for pixel-perfect placement.
- **Resizable windows:** drag the corner of any settings window, and the window you opened last always stays on top.
- **Profiles per character:** one shared profile for everyone, or an own profile for any character – for all addons at once (**/forever profile**).
- **Share your interface:** export all your Forever Core settings as one text and import them on another PC or for a friend (**/forever export**).
